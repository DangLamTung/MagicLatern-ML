film_sim colour calibration
===========================

Input: X-Rite ColorChecker Classic (sRGB approximations), 24 patches,
720x480, converted to UYVY with ML's rgb2yuv422_rec601 coefficients.
Error metric: CIEDE2000 between reference sRGB and the measured patch.
(dE00 ~1 is just visible, ~2-3 is a clear shift, >5 is wrong.)

1. Chroma convention of the 6D LiveView buffers
------------------------------------------------
  signed  U/V (int8, neutral 0x00)  mean dE00  0.86  max  2.11
  offset  U/V (neutral 0x80)        mean dE00 51.05  max 86.83
  => signed wins, matching imgconv.c (COERCE(...,-128,127) + UYVY_PACK).
     The module uses signed chroma.

2. Round-trip through the UYVY path (no film applied)
----------------------------------------------------
  RGB -> UYVY -> RGB               mean dE00  0.53  max  1.38
  (4:2:2 subsampling and 8-bit rounding set this floor)

2b. Is the filter itself colour-neutral?
----------------------------------------
  Neutral, strength 100, grain 0    mean dE00  0.00  max  0.00
  (measured against the pipeline input, so the 8-bit UYVY round-trip
   above cancels out and only the filter's own error remains)
  => bit-exact identity: the filter introduces no colour error; every
     other number below is the film look doing what it was written to do.
